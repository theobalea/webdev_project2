Manchester United and Cristiano Ronaldo

One day, my dad brought home some Manchester United pencils from a business trip he went on. From that day on, my brother and I became big fans of Manchester United. Cristiano Ronaldo has been a significant part of the club's history and success, and despite being too young to remember his moments there, I loved following and watching him play the more I grew up. His work ethic and dedication inspire me to work hard not just in soccer, but to become successful at whatever you do in life. He is my idol.

About the Club

  • Founded in 1878
  • Home stadium: Old Trafford in Manchester, England, nicknamed the Theatre of Dreams
  • Known as the Red Devils
  • Winner of 20 English league titles
  • Winner of 3 UEFA Champions League titles
  • First English club to win the Premier League, FA cup, and Champions League in a single season (known as the Treble)

Ronaldo at United

  1. 2003: Joins United from Sporting CP as an 18-year-old and takes the number 7 shirt.
  2. 2007 to 2009: Helps win three Premier League titles in a row.
  3. 2008: Wins the Champions League and the Ballon d'Or (awarded to the best player in the world that year).
  4. 2009: Wins the Puskas award (awarded to the best goal scored in a calendar year), and transfers to Real Madrid for a then-world record fee of £80 million.
  5. 2021: Returns to Manchester United for a second spell.

Why He Inspires Me

Ronaldo is famous for outworking everyone around him. As a student athlete who trains more than 20 hours a week and still has to keep up in class, I take that lesson seriously: talent matters, but discipline and consistency decide the outcome. I aspire to play just like him, and every time I score a goal, I immediately am thinking to do his signature celebration.
