Welcome to my Home Page!

I am a second-year Applied Cybersecurity and Information Technology student and a member of the men's soccer team at Illinois Institute of Technology. I am from Peoria, Illinois, but my parents are originally from Romania. I grew up speaking Romanian at home, and I usually get to visit Romania every two years.

My Soccer Story

I have played soccer since I was just 5 years old. Over the years I have been a player, a referee, and a youth camp volunteer, and each role taught me something different about the game. I have also had the chance to play for many different teams, even playing in St. Louis for a year. I drove around 300 total miles each day I went to practice or had a game, which was at least 2-3 times a week. As you can tell, I have a strong passion for soccer, and I am very grateful to now be able to play for Illinois Tech while also studying Cybersecurity.

Travel Experiences

With my parents being from Romania, I have had the opportunity to visit the country multiple times. I always enjoy seeing where they grew up and visiting all my relatives that I really don't get to see often. Aside from Romania, I have also traveled to several other European countries. My three favorites were:

  • Italy
  • Greece
  • Switzerland

In Italy, the towns were beautiful and the food was amazing. Greece had the best beaches. The water was so clear and warm. Switzerland was extra special because my uncle lives there. It is really hard to beat the mountains of Switzerland. The hikes and lakes were breathtaking.

Why I Chose Cybersecurity

When I was in high school, I didn't quite know what I wanted to do, but I was always interested in technology and how it works. After researching and learning about Cybersecurity specifically, I became more and more curious about what it is and what Cybersecurity professionals do. Many people think it is just hacking, but there is much more to it than that. One day, I hope to become a Cybersecurity Engineer, where I can configure my own security protocols and help defend my organization's data and network.
